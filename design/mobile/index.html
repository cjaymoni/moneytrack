moneytrack — mobile

Cocoa Grade Stencil. Every screen below is the real markup at 390×844, running on the shared tokens. Add ?theme=dark to any screen’s URL for the dark scheme.

Navigation, sheets and controls here are painted stand-ins, not a component library. In Flutter they become Material 3 on Android and HIG on iOS — see ADAPTIVE.md for the per-component mapping. The world lends type, palette, density and the stamp, and nothing else.

All figures, merchants and dates are invented sample data for design purposes. There are no real users and no real transactions.
